## Grass species icons
<img src="/assets/images/rice.png" class="db ba w-100" width="18%" align="center" /> <img src="/assets/images/wheat.png" class="db ba w-100" width="18%" align="center" /> <img src="/assets/images/foxtail_millet.png" class="db ba w-100" width="18%" align="center" /> <img src="/assets/images/finger_millet.png" class="db ba w-100" width="18%" align="center" /> <img src="/assets/images/perennial_ryegrass.png" class="db ba w-100" width="18%" align="center" />

I created species icons for five grasses representing host plants of the rice blast disease, for my [2023 paper on transposable element dynamics in _Magnaporthe oryzae_](https://doi.org/10.1093/gbe/evad206). From left to right: rice (_Oryza_), wheat (_Triticum_), foxtail (_Setaria_), goosegrass (_Eleusine_), and ryegrass (_Lolium_).

## Hawai‘i Academy of Science STEM Poster Contest 2017
<img src="/assets/images/hssef_poster_2017.jpg" class="db ba w-100" width="60%" align="right" style="margin: 3%" />
