About Me

I am a Biomolecular Engineering & Bioinformatics PhD Candidate at UC Santa Cruz in the Corbett-Detig Lab.

As an evolutionary genomicist and bioinformatician, my research uses landscape-scale comparative genomics to inform biodiversity conservation. My work spans diverse taxa, including fish, mammals, birds, plants, herps, and invertebrates in California, as well as fungal pathogens affecting a native tree in Hawai‘i.
